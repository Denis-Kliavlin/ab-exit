## How to Read This Book

The document is universal. **Parts I–III** describe the protocol for **any country**: the formula is tied to the median wage of a given economy, and the philosophy, theory and responses to criticism do not depend on jurisdiction. The empirical cases (chapter 5) draw on data from several countries — that is an evidence base, not an attachment to one country.

**Part IV** holds the country implementations: concrete legal routes, pilot jurisdictions and calculations for individual countries. The United States is one country case among others, and it comes last.

Each section is self-contained — you can read it straight through as a book (using the "next/previous" buttons at the bottom of each page) or jump to what you need via the table of contents below.
