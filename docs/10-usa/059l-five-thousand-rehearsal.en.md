# The US: the "Trump $5,000" Campaign as a Rehearsal for the Protocol — Eight Stages and a Forecast

Trace all the stages and forecast what they may say about the protocol and how the campaign will go." *How to read. For the first time in US history a number "so much for everyone" ran the whole cycle from launch to resolution in front of everyone in eight weeks. It is the only field experiment close to the protocol, and the chapter takes it as a template: the same stage — what was said about Trump, what will be said about the protocol, how the protocol differs at that point. There are exactly three differences, all in the protocol's favour but one: legality must be spoken first.*

## 3. The fourth stage — the architect's correction: the number acquires salesmen

The assistant wrote: "the polls — the same profile: about half for, the young higher, the parties mirrored". The architect: "Here you are wrong — read the repo. If the burned see the ×2 vote and the exit of the apathetic, they will advertise the protocol themselves on every corner and in their networks — reposting the calculation for a family of three or four and what it can buy, and saying: 'surely Americans won't sell out for a new fridge, right?' — honestly, looking up from the phone, with a smile."

The error is the "before" yardstick: the poll profile was carried over from a number that had no salesman except Trump himself. Nobody campaigned for the $5,000: Republicans disowned it (stage 5), Democrats attacked it, Trump repeated it alone (stage 6). For the protocol two armies with opposite motives will campaign for free. *The burned* — seeing ×2 and the exit of the apathetic, will carry it themselves: it is the first offer in which they exist (1f §6b), and they have networks, numbers and a place in history (059g §4). *The takers* — will repost the family calculation: four adults at $800 — $3,200 for one election, and what that buys; it is Luntz's cheque (040an §4) in a format shared with one tap. And the format is found: "surely Americans won't sell out for a new fridge, right?" — at once a mockery of the myth of the sacred vote (059c §2) and an advertisement; there is no reply to it, because any reply confirms either the price or the hypocrisy. It is the lottery with a tip for a ticket (040ap §4), only sold by the players themselves.

So the fourth stage for the protocol is not "about half" but growth from one reading to the next: Trump's 48 % with zero salesmen is a floor, not a profile. What to test: the share of reposts with the family calculation against the share of reposts with "bribery" in the first two weeks — that is the measurement of "who heard" (040an §7).

## 4. Three points where the protocol's campaign is harder than Trump's

1. **Legality — say it first.** "How is this legal" is rhetoric for Trump; for the protocol it is a real question: 18 U.S.C. § 597 prohibits paying for voting and for abstaining in federal elections (059c §4, Hasen; 006 §252). Hence the start is state elections and below (048m art. 8), through a state constitutional initiative, as in the Missouri scenario; the federal level needs an amendment by Congress. Say it before the opponent does, in the first text.
2. **Arithmetic without "it pays for itself".** Trump was forgiven "the math doesn't add up" because his number dies. The protocol will not be forgiven: show the sum per election as a line in the state budget beside the line it replaces. Otherwise the third stage becomes the last.
3. **Trust after Trump.** By 2027 "so much for everyone" in the US is a burned phrase: promised twice, paid never. The protocol wins on this only if from day one it says: that is exactly why not a promise but a ballot line that you adopt yourselves.

## 5. What the protocol takes ready-made from Trump's campaign

Proof of demand: 48 % for at zero elaboration and zero salesmen. Proof that a number travels every channel without a budget, hostile ones included (040aq §4). The figure for the young — 61 % for the money: the position on the young with a long horizon (040ai §4, 040al §4) should be tested on exactly that number. And the ten critics of 059c §4, who will say about the protocol what they said about Trump — the answers are already written.

## 6. Forecast and check

- 3 November 2026: Trump's number dies on any outcome — the test of stage 8.
- The protocol's first state campaign (the Missouri scenario): stages 2 and 3 pass within the first week in the same words as Trump's; stage 4 shows growth in support between the first and second readings, not a plateau; stage 5 — the first defector from the second echelon of one of the parties before the end of signature-gathering.
- If stage 4 gives a plateau at Trump's level, the correction of §3 is wrong, and that must be recorded.

## Weak point

The chronology is from press retellings, not primary sources; the polls are one window (11–14 September). The forecast of §2 is a transfer by analogy: Trump's number was promised by a candidate, the protocol's is put up by an initiative, and some reactions (the party split, legality) may go differently. The correction of §3 is the architect's forecast; the measurement is the first two weeks of the first campaign.
