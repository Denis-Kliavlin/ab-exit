# AB-EXIT and the Capture of Institutions: an Answer to Ernst Fraenkel

**Chapter:** 06 — Structural Hypocrisy and the Arsenal of the Elites
**File:** 06_034 · v1 · 12 June 2026 (sessions 33–36)

## Purpose

Ernst Fraenkel ("The Dual State", 1941; post-war neo-pragmatic pluralism) is the most dangerous institutional critic of AB-EXIT. He strikes not at the economics (that flank is closed by the section on Henry George) but at the mechanism's stability over time: what will happen to AB-EXIT in the hands of a bad-faith power. The section's result: of his three blows two break on facts and the text of the protocol; the third is accepted as an engineering requirement of constitutional level.

## Fraenkel's optics

**Normenstaat vs Maßnahmenstaat.** Analysing Nazism, Fraenkel showed the coexistence of the normative state (laws, predictable rules) and the prerogative state (arbitrariness "by expediency") — and that the prerogative can always swallow the norm. **Pluralism:** a healthy democracy is institutionalised open conflict of groups within an "undisputed core" of common values. His criterion: does a reform strengthen the norm and open conflict — or create a zone of prerogative and muffle conflict.

## What Fraenkel would approve

A formula dividend changeable only by referendum — pure Normenstaat against discretion. Direct payment bypassing the official-distributor — a narrowing of the existing prerogative zone. Recognition of the legitimacy of non-participation — anti-totalitarian (totalitarianism demands 99 % turnout).

## Three blows

**Blow 1 — "you have created a syringe".** A payment register tied to political behaviour is ready infrastructure for a future autocrat: reward the loyal, punish the disloyal "by budgetary necessity". Weimar with impeccable laws generated prerogative through Article 48.

**Blow 2 — "you muffle conflict instead of institutionalising it".** Removing 30–40 % of citizens from electoral conflict for money = depoliticisation; unrepresented conflict accumulates outside the rules and breaks through outside the rules.

**Blow 3 — "equality of the vote is taken out of the undisputed core".** Variable weight moves one person, one vote from "not up for discussion" to "depends on choice"; once shifted, it is easier to shift further.

## Analysis

**Blow 2 breaks on the empty chair.** The argument presupposes that the citizens being removed conduct a represented conflict. There is none: 48 % left by themselves, for years, for free — their non-participation today is a mute zero the system ignores. AB-EXIT does not remove them from the public sphere — it records an exit that has already happened and turns the mute zero into a registered, paid, reversible act. A person who previously simply did not come makes an official choice B — this is a growth of representation. Whoever wants conflict refuses the money and gets a strengthened vote: the channel is open, and in it are those who really want to be there.

**Blow 3 softens to a frame.** AB-EXIT makes equality activatable: everyone is offered an identical choice (equality of opportunity); weight is the consequence of a free reversible decision, not of status by birth or income (unlike historical qualifications). The honest price remains: "equality that requires activation is no longer an undisputed core." The answer: the reversibility of every cycle keeps the principle closer to the core than any irreversible qualification; everything is introduced and abolished only by referendum.

**Blow 1 — remains, and rightly so.** Capture of the payment mechanism is a real risk that cannot be argued away. The answer is constructional, safeguards of constitutional level: (1) a formula lock — parameters change only by referendum; (2) a public payment register — selective "suspension" is visible immediately; (3) an autonomous trust — the executive does not touch the money; (4) protection from election interference (§44). Additionally: the risk of budget capture already exists now — in the form of opaque administrative rent without formula or register. AB-EXIT does not create the risk; it replaces an existing opaque capture with a transparent mechanism that is harder to capture. (Cf. §13.12: every incentive machine required the completion of its frame — the safeguards are built into the core from day one.)

## Quantitative balance: risk against signal cleansing

The risk and the benefit live in different tenses: the cleansing is immediate and every cycle; capture is deferred and conditional (a coincidence: an autocrat in power × the formula lock broken × the register broken × the referendum threshold broken; each factor < 1).

A model estimate of the cleansing (method: segmentation §17, Alaska PFD empirics §14, Shapley §13):

| | Current system | After AB-EXIT |
|---|---|---|
| Vote | ~52 % | ~35–50 % |
| Share of the aware (pragmatists+ideologues) among voters | ~40 % | ~65–70 % |
| Signal-to-noise ratio | ~0.67 | ~2.0 |

**Signal-to-noise grows 2–3 times; the share of consciously cast deciding votes: from ~40 % to ~65–70 % (+15–30 pp).** Caveats: in poor economies some pragmatists will take the dividend (the lower bound); "aware" = resistant to manipulation, not "objectively right"; manipulators adapt, but targeting an aware electorate is more expensive and slower.

## Fraenkel's verdict (reconstruction)

> "The idea narrows arbitrariness in the present — that is its merit, and no small one. But it creates a payment institution tied to political behaviour and moves equality of the vote from the undisputed core into the zone of choice. In the hands of a good-faith democracy it is a cure; in the hands of a prerogative state — a ready syringe. Build it, but build in safeguards against yourselves."

The project accepts this as a technical specification: the safeguards are part of the core, of constitutional level, designed before the mechanism itself.
