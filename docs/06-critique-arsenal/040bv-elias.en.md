# 40bv. Norbert Elias: Ten Theses — Self-Restraint Gets a Price, Chains of Foresight a Unit, Figuration a Number, and the Monopoly on Violence a Hall That Counts

**Chapter:** 06 — The Structural Hypocrisy of Criticism and the Arsenal of the Elites
**File:** 06_040bv · v1 · 7 November 2026
**Source:** the architect's question of 7 November 2026 (analysis of a Gemini chat: "what would Elias say"). Norbert Elias: *The Civilizing Process* (1939; Fremdzwang → Selbstzwang, the lengthening of chains of interdependence and foresight, the monopoly on violence and taxation), *The Court Society* (1969), *What Is Sociology?* (1970; figurations, the balance of power as a relation, not a thing), *The Society of Individuals* (1987), *The Germans* (1989; decivilising relapses). Quotations are paraphrased.

---

*How to read. Elias described the same quantity as Gumilev (040bp), only from the other end: not a "surplus of impulse" but the "length of the chain of foresight" — how far a person sees consequences and restrains himself now for their sake. He described the quantity across centuries and never measured it once. The chapter tests ten theses and three blows: "you turn civic duty into a transaction — a decivilising relapse", "self-restraint cannot be bought", "the monopoly on violence stays with whoever holds it".*

## 1. Ten theses

| No. | Elias's thesis | What is true today | What the protocol does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Civilisation is the passage from external constraint to self-constraint | True; but self-constraint is described, not measured | Refusing D for the vote is self-constraint with a sum and a date; the share refusing, by district, is the first scale ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md); [040bp §5](040bp-gumilev.md)) |
| 2 | The lengthening of chains of foresight: a person sees further and restrains the impulse | The horizon is a property of the person, not of age ([1g](../01-introduction/001g-objections-index.md); [016](../04-electoral-dynamics/016-age-economics.md)) | The rule asks exactly this: an immediate reward or a deferred one; the answer is the length of the chain, expressed in behaviour |
| 3 | Figuration: power is not a thing but a balance in a web of interdependence | Yes; but the balance is invisible, reckoned from a turnout that was bought | The balance becomes a number: everyone knows his weight depends on how many left ([1b: 1/(N − M)](../01-introduction/001b-essence.md)); the figuration is transparent |
| 4 | The monopoly on violence and taxation is the core of the state | Yes; and both monopolies are held by whoever is brought in to vote | Taxes and the payment are computed from one base — the median (048m art. 3); "tanks do not vote", power turns to the centre ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)) |
| 5 | Court society: status rests on nearness to the centre, not on deeds | The political class lives on nearness to the budget | A line for the citizen, not for the apparatus (art. 14); the "helicopter rich man" who lives on nearness loses his hidden transfers ([040m, ninth rule](040m-cold-reader-audit.md)) |
| 6 | Shame and embarrassment are the instrument of civilisation: the threshold of shame rises | Non-voting is a shame; "civic duty" is a reproach | Non-voting stops being a shame and becomes a choice with money; the apathetic gains dignity and peace ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)) |
| 7 | Decivilising relapses are possible (Germany in the 1930s): when the chains snap, violence returns | Pape: tens of millions "support violence" in surveys | A free answer in a survey and refusing D for one's own rage are different quantities; the combustible material leaves for the payment, the spark finds nobody to ignite (§4) |
| 8 | Established and outsiders: group charisma and group disgrace hold the boundary | Party machines hold the boundary by busing | The boundary loses its material: the brought-in has left, the outsider with a product enters without permission ([048n](../08-implementation/048n-underdog-questions.md)) |
| 9 | The society of individuals: the "we" and the "I" are a balance that shifts historically | The balance has shifted towards the "I", yet the political decision is still taken by the "we" of the party | The political decision moves from the party into everyone's head ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)): "is my vote worth more than D to me or not?" |
| 10 | The process is unplanned: it is the result of interweaving intentions, not of anyone's design | True; and it is the main argument against "designed" reforms ([040bn](040bn-hayek.md)) | The rule does not design society — it designs one question and gives the interweaving of intentions a counter; what comes of it nobody plans (arts. 2–3: p is set by referendum) |

## 2. The first blow: "you turn civic duty into a transaction — that is the decivilising relapse: the snapping of the chain that bound a person to the state without money"

The answer is a fork. Accept it whole: any monetary transaction between citizen and state in place of duty is a snapping of the chain. Then the chain is already snapped, and long ago: half do not come and are bound to the state by nothing — neither duty nor transaction ([040aa](040aa-runciman.md)); and the other half is bound by a hidden transaction — a benefit, an indexation, a package, a bus ([057h §2.6](../09-other-countries/057h-serbia-2026.md)). Either the argument is false and a transaction is not a snapping; or it is true — and then the rule does not snap the chain but for the first time makes it explicit and the same for all. In Elias's own account civilisation proceeds not by abolishing interests but by lengthening them: the merchant restrains himself for the sake of profit a year away. The rule lengthens the chain in exactly that way: take now, or keep the vote for power a term away — a question about the horizon, put to everyone with a sum attached. That is not a relapse but what he called "the lengthening of the chain", put on a counter.

## 3. The second blow: "self-restraint cannot be bought; whoever restrains himself for money does not restrain himself"

True — and the rule does not buy self-restraint. It pays for its absence and measures its presence. Whoever took showed no self-restraint, and that is honestly recorded; whoever refused showed it against a known sum — and that is the first case in which Elias's quantity can be compared across districts, countries and cycles. Here the chapter meets [040bp](040bp-gumilev.md): Gumilev's passionarity and Elias's self-restraint are one and the same refusal described from two sides (surplus of impulse / length of foresight), and before the rule neither had an instrument. What remains: the scale measures behaviour, not motive; horizon and vanity give the same refusal ([040bo §3](040bo-montaigne.md)) — the hall does not become wiser, it becomes a hall of those who counted.

## 4. The third blow: "the monopoly on violence stays with whoever holds it; your hall will count, and the soldier will carry out the order"

The answer from the register: "suppression without a referendum loses its executor" — the rank and file of the army and police have themselves taken the payment, and "we cancel it" is not a thing one says to a soldier ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)); "power turns to the centre, not to force: turnout is among the stayers, tanks do not vote". Elias himself described why the monopoly rests not on weapons but on figuration — on thousands of people performing their roles every day; the rule changes every one of those roles with one question, and the holder of the monopoly finds he holds it over a hall that no longer exists. On "combustible material" and decivilising relapse — [040bs](040bs-paine.md) and the analysis of Pape: free support for violence in a survey and refusing D for its sake are different numbers; nobody has measured the second, and it is smaller by multiples.

## 5. What Elias gives the protocol

A unit for a quantity the repository has so far described in words — "horizon", "the burned": the length of the chain of foresight, measured by the refusal of a sum with a date. The concept of figuration — power as a relation, which the rule turns into a number. And the main historical argument: civilisation advanced not by abolishing interest but by lengthening it — exactly what one question with a sum does.

*Addition of 9 November 2026.* The same quantity with the opposite sign — Genis's observation after the 2026 election: freedom is given away easily, by voting for "peace" or "freedom" from the lips of one who brings the opposite. This is a chain of foresight of zero length in a vote that cost its giver nothing ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md): support without a price is unmeasured).

| chains of foresight | yes | a question about the horizon to everyone | — |
| figuration | yes | the balance becomes a number | — |
| monopoly on violence and taxation | yes | one base for tax and payment; tanks do not vote | force cancels everything; no defence against a coup |
| court society | yes | nearness loses its transfers | — |
| shame | yes | non-voting is a choice, not a shame | — |

| established/outsiders | yes | a boundary without material | — |
| "we"/"I" | yes | the decision in everyone's head | — |
| unplannedness | yes | the question is designed, not the result | — |

## Weak point

Elias is paraphrased and much simplified: his "chains of foresight" are a property of the social web, not of the individual, and the chapter transfers them to a personal choice, which he himself did not do. "Decivilising relapse" is applied to today's surveys on violence by analogy, not by his method. The scale "refusal of D = length of horizon" is the author's proposal, not Elias's; it merges horizon, vanity and income into one refusal, and they cannot be separated without a measurement.
