*How to read. Penn is the only one of the eight technologists with a measuring instrument of his own: a monthly national poll. So his arguments are about measurement, and the answer to them is one: measurement is not an objection but a plan, and the instrument for it already stands in Penn's own shop. With this chapter the technologists' series (040ak–040ar) closes.*

## 0. Penn's craft

Clinton's pollster in 1996, co-author of triangulation with Morris (040ao), discoverer of the "soccer moms" — a micro-group that decided an election because it was addressed first. Hillary Clinton's chief strategist in 2008. *Microtrends*: the nation consists of hundreds of one-per-cent groups, and elections are won by whoever finds his own. Owner of the Harris Poll and head of Stagwell; the Harvard CAPS/Harris poll comes out monthly. In 2026: the Democrats are "in ruins", a wipeout forecast; the Republicans ahead among likely voters 51:49 for the first time since October 2025; "voters want to hear about inflation, fraud and AI".

## 1. There are no "burned" — there are a hundred one-per-cent groups

**Argument.** One number addresses none of them; the electorate is a mosaic of microtrends, and a protocol addressed to "the burned" is addressed to nobody.

**Answer.** The number addresses not by lifestyle but by horizon and income — and cuts all hundred groups across (040al §4). Penn's hundred microtrends are the moving centre of 040ao §1, which takes B; the standing centre is exactly what one number assembles, because it has no microtrend — it has a position. And his own "soccer moms" prove our move: the group addressed first decides the election; "the burned" are the group nobody addressed (1f §6b).

**Residual.** None.

## 2. The polls will show that people want the money but do not trust it, and support collapses once the condition is explained

**Argument.** Trump's 48 to 40 (059c §5) is "we want a cheque"; ask "would you take money for not voting", and support falls.

**Answer.** The 48 % came with zero salesmen and no rule (059l §3) — a floor, not a profile. The "condition" is not a condition but the taker's own choice, reversible at any election (048m arts. 5, 16). And this is his own product: two questions separately — "would you take" and "would you stay" (048n, "measuring the question: a poll is cheaper than an ad"), and the Harris Poll is the first to sell it, because the daily poll "how many will take" (040an §3) becomes a national number, and someone has to count it.

**Residual.** None.

## 3. The soft centre decides — pragmatic, risk-averse; a radical rule frightens it

**Argument.** For thirty years Penn has sold "the centre" as caution: soccer moms, moderates, "don't rock the boat".

**Answer.** Penn's soft centre is the centre by motion: "the apathetic" and "the meek" (040ao §1), and they are not frightened, they take. The centre by position is not soft — it judges by substance (1f §6b), and radicalism is indifferent to it; arithmetic matters. Penn is right that the centre decides and, like Morris, wrong about which.

**Residual.** None.

## 4. Everything must be tested: an untested message loses — and the architect's move

**Argument.** Penn's whole school: test before you speak; the protocol has never been tested.

**The assistant's answer.** It will be tested, but at others' expense — the burned will hire the best (040an §7b), and the number tests itself by the daily poll. The one focus group needed is of those who stay (040an §7). Here Penn is not an opponent but a contractor.

**The architect's move.** "Within one or two years one can find a city for tests, find sponsors and run a super-fast simple test. Although even in focus groups the result will differ from the usual so much that the dispute will be not about the sign of the result but about whether governance improves ×2 or ×4. The first $10,000 for a poll will be very easy to find, I think." This turns the argument from an objection into a plan. The first ten thousand is the "chip in for yourselves" contribution (040ap §4) in its first form; the city and the sponsors in a year or two (048l, the courtyard pilot). And the main thing — the effect size: an ordinary focus group looks for a five-point difference and argues about the sign; here the result will be so unlike anything Penn ever measured that the argument will be not whether it works but whether it is ×2 or ×4. A rare case in political technology where a measurement is needed not to prove an effect but to size it.

**Residual.** None — the residual became a task for the pilot.

## 5. Reputation: a brand linked to "paid not to vote" is toxic, and corporations will not pay

**Argument.** Penn ran corporate reputations at Burson for twenty years; from his vantage point the protocol is a reputational risk, and there will be no money for it.

**Answer.** Corporate money is not needed: the takers fund themselves (040ap §4), the donors are the burned with a place in history (059g §4). And brands follow the sixty per cent just as parties do (059l, stage 5, speed): what is toxic is not paying, but having come out against money for half the hall.

**Residual.** None.

## 6. What Penn gives the protocol

*The instrument.* A monthly poll with a ready panel, where one question — "would you take or stay" — turns the protocol's national number (040an §3) into regular statistics before any referendum.

*The method.* Microtrends as a way to find who inside the band around the median takes (040al §4): horizon markers instead of lifestyle markers — a task for the pilot beside Dalton's instrument (040aj §6).

*The precedent.* The "soccer moms": the group addressed first decides the election.

## Weak point

Penn has not written about the price of the vote; a reconstruction from *Microtrends* and the 2026 polls; the column on the Democrats' wipeout is dated from retellings. The effect size "×2 or ×4" in §4 is the architect's expectation, not a measurement: the first poll and the pilot will show what the argument is about. The technologists' series (040ak–040ar) closes at eight chapters: their arguments are simple because they are optimised for the hall (040am), and the one hard argument turned out to be not about the mechanism but about adoption (040al §4) — and that one was removed by the move about the band around the median.
