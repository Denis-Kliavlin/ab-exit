## 2. The first blow: "the combustible material is a new breed of people — a hundred million supporters of violence"

The architect's move, 8 November 2026: "Pape's combustible material is the burned, to whom the system left no channel, not a new breed of people. Yes, they want to change this by any means, but AB-EXIT gives them a strengthened vote, and most of them will take part in elections in order to vote — and also to increase the payment and remove more of the apathetic, so that the vote becomes heavier still."

Analysis. The burned is one who tried and drowned in the votes of the apathetic without a horizon; that is where the rule came from ([1e](../01-introduction/001e-by-role.md): Russia, 2014). "By any means" — because there was no lawful means of out-voting: the brought-in are always more. The rule gives a lawful means — a vote of 1/(N − M) and a date — and so Pape's 40 and 60 million read not as a threat but as a measurement of demand for a channel: the largest reserve of fuel for a referendum in the country. The move's second step is the burned's interest in the price: the higher D, the more take and the heavier the remaining vote; the taker wants it higher too. Two opposite groups pull the price the same way — a dispute over distribution, rare in politics, in which the sides agree. The limit: p is changed only by a referendum of all (048m art. 3); the hall cannot raise it alone, only persuade; and D is paid from the same median from which the stayers' tax is computed ([1i: a dispute over distribution is settled by price](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)). What remains of the blow: "I support violence" in a survey and refusing D for one's own rage are different quantities; nobody has measured the second, and it is smaller by multiples — by how much, only a priced poll will show.

## 3. The second blow: "the most dangerous moment is the transfer of power; your rule does not abolish it"

True, it does not. But Pape defends certification with a moratorium on armed agents, while the protocol makes certification verifiable by anyone in a minute: records = votes + payments + neither (art. 10), and whoever took the money holds a document showing his vote is cancelled. January 6 was a dispute over a count neither side could prove; the rule removes not the crowd at the Capitol but the reason for which it can be gathered. [048i](../08-implementation/048i-three-channels-parallel-count.md), [1i: the unprovability of the absence of fraud disappears](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md).

## 4. The third blow: "what to do — three firebreaks; that is realism, and your referendum is not"

Pape's three firebreaks are requests: to Congress, to governors, to mediators, and from the citizen — a letter to his politician. That is an appeal to the hall that accumulated the combustible material, asking it to behave differently while the material grows. The repository's realism is different: the rule asks nobody — a referendum adopts it ([048m art. 14](../08-implementation/048m-charter.md)), the carrier needs no permission ([048n](../08-implementation/048n-underdog-questions.md)), and the burned gets, instead of a letter, a vote with weight and a date. Pape is right that not every spark can be removed; the repository answers that it is not the sparks that need removing.

## 5. What Pape gives the protocol

A regular measurement of the combustible material in millions — the only data series of its kind, and it shows growing demand for a channel. The wildfire model (material — spark — wind), in which the protocol has its own place: it is the only instrument that changes the quantity of material. And the exact observation that the transfer of power is the most dangerous moment, which points to the rule's most important unit: verification of the count by payments.

## Weak point

The figures of 40 and 60 million are Pape's own surveys; the video does not give the question wording, and the chapter has no independent re-check. The rendering of violent populism as "militant populism" is the architect's; Pape is not a theorist of populism, and the chapter does not attribute to him a defence of it. "The burned, not a new breed" is the architect's move, supported by the origin of the rule but not by a measurement of who those millions are. The equilibrium of p with two groups pulling the price up is a calculation from the charter, not an observation.
