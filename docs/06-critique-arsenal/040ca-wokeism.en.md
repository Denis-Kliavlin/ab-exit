# 40ca. Wokeism: Five Parameters — the Group as a Type, the Free Currency of Status, Rights and Support in One Package, Enforcement past the Hall, Zero Sum; and What of It Survives a Price

**Chapter:** 06 — The Structural Hypocrisy of Criticism and the Arsenal of the Elites
**File:** 06_040ca · v1 · 13 November 2026
**Source:** the architect's question of 13 November 2026 ("check wokeism by its main parameters and how it is commensurable with AB-EXIT"). The term is contested and used as a label by both sides; the chapter takes not the label but the construction — five properties the phenomenon has under any definition. The analysis is deliberately symmetrical across camps: the rule does not take a side, and the text must not either.

---

*How to read. Wokeism here is not an object of judgment but a material the rule recounts: a politics of recognition built on the free vote and the free statement. The chapter shows that as a programme it is incommensurable with the protocol (content against form), while as a mechanism it is commensurable on all five parameters, and that after the rule its package of demands splits into three parts with different fates: rights remain entire, support becomes a question for the payers, status loses its cheapest channel. The same happens, row by row, to its mirror — right-wing populism.*

## 1. Five parameters and what corresponds to them in the rule

| № | Parameter | Wokeism | AB-EXIT | Where in detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The unit of politics | a group by an ascribed trait — race, sex, orientation; the trait is forever, the person is an instance of a type | a state, not a type: "apathetic", "burned", "deferring" — a position at this election, changing every cycle; the rule knows no traits, only the choice | [1h](../01-introduction/001h-glossary.md), [1f](../01-introduction/001f-core-electorate.md) |
| 2 | The currency | recognition and moral status: words, symbols, language, representation; a free currency — a statement costs the speaker nothing | paid: the vote costs D; a statement that survived the offer of money is no longer a tribe but a conviction | [040at §3](040at-achen-bartels.md), [040by §5](040by-pinker.md) |
| 3 | The type of demand | rights and support fused into one package: equal rights + resources, quotas, rules of speech — all as society's debt | separated: "do no harm" — unconditional, that is the constitution and the courts, the rule does not touch them; "support" — only by convincing those who stayed to pay | [013e §5](../03-theory/013e-economics-in-politics.md), [1g](../01-introduction/001g-objections-index.md): "suppresses the votes of minorities" |
| 4 | The mechanism of enforcement | not the hall but institutions: HR departments, universities, editorial boards, social sanction; the barriers are held by people with names, not by a majority | decisions return to a hall with a price; a barrier held by a person with a name does not stand against a question of money for the family | [048n](../08-implementation/048n-underdog-questions.md): barriers are not symmetrical |
| 5 | The sum | redistribution of status — zero-sum: recognition of one group reads as demotion of another | the rule's question is a balance with mutual exclusion; the zero-sum mindset in a voter is the same as in one who demands the exclusion of outsiders | [040bz §2](040bz-seven-arguments-referendum.md), [056d §5b](../09-other-countries/056d-russia-opposition-deadlock.md) |

Factual footing, from memory and unchecked: a USA Today/Ipsos poll (2023) — about 56 % of Americans understand the word positively ("aware of injustice"), about 39 % negatively; at the 2024 election young men and Hispanics shifted right — the reverse stroke of the same free machine.

## 2. Incommensurable as programmes, commensurable as mechanisms

Wokeism is content: what to decide. The protocol is form: who decides and at what price. The rule is neither left nor right and cuts the electorate across, not along ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)); so wokeism is for it neither ally nor opponent but one of the materials the rule recounts. To compare them as two programmes is a category error; to compare them as two mechanisms is possible on every row of the table, and in every row the difference is the same: one has a free currency, the other a paid one.

## 3. What survives a price: the package splits into three parts

After the rule, the demands that wokeism submits as one package go to three addresses.

- **Rights** — remain entire. They are held by the constitution and the courts, the rule does not touch them; comparison with the existing system gives "no worse" ([013e §5](../03-theory/013e-economics-in-politics.md)).
- **Support** — quotas, programmes, money — becomes a question for the hall of payers. Either the group convinced those who refused D — and received; or it did not. Help for the picture at the people's expense stops passing the hall by, because there is no longer a way past the hall.
- **Status** — language, symbols, rules of speech — loses its cheapest channel. Its engine was the free vote and the free statement: those who like for free do not go to vote ([040bd §4](040bd-q-citizens-are-rational.md)), and those who go now pay. With a price, status is left with persuasion alone — slow and honest.

## 4. The mirror: right-wing populism is the same phenomenon of the free vote

Wokeism and right-wing populism are symmetrical on all five rows: the group as type ("the people", "the natives"), a free currency (pride, grievance), a package of rights and support ("our jobs"), enforcement through captured institutions, zero sum ("they take from us"). Both construct "the people" from a chain of grievances against an enemy ([040bx](040bx-laclau-mouffe.md)); both live in the zone where one may believe anything because it costs nothing ([040by](040by-pinker.md)); both rest on an assembly of "againsts" that falls apart at the first price ([059p §3b](../10-usa/059p-third-force-demand.md)). The rule strikes both alike and for exactly that reason belongs to neither: a supporter of either camp who refused D for his position is already a conviction, and with convictions a contest of ideas is possible ([040at §3](040at-achen-bartels.md)).

## 5. The blow from this side: "one price, different incomes — the hall will turn whiter and older"

The strongest argument of a wokeism supporter against the rule: D is the same for all, incomes are not; the poorest groups, disproportionately minorities, will take the money more often, and the hall will shift toward the white, the old and the well-off. The answer is already recorded in [1g](../01-introduction/001g-objections-index.md) ("suppresses the votes of the poor and of minorities"): nobody loses the vote, both rights are kept and chosen anew every cycle; suppressed today is the one whose vote is brought by bus and a package of benefits and whose opinion nobody asks; the comparison must be with the existing system, where the turnout of the poor is lower anyway and nobody pays them. This chapter's addition: the question "who will take" is a question about a state, not a trait: the needy takes at once, "the deferring" at the end of the window, the stayer does not take, and the trait has nothing to do with it ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md): the timing of the choice). The honest remainder: the share of takers by group is a measurement, not a deduction; if it turns out overwhelming, the dispute will be about the size of p, not about the right.

## 6. Summary

| Parameter | Wokeism | Rule | What remains |
|---|---|---|---|
| unit | group as type | state | — |
| currency | free status | paid vote | persuasion instead of status |
| demand | rights + support | rights unconditional, support by convincing payers | — |
| enforcement | institutions past the hall | a hall with a price | — |
| sum | zero | balance | — |
| mirror | right-wing populism | the same | — |
| blow | the hall turns whiter | state, not trait; 1g | share of takers by group — a measurement |

## Weak point

The five-property definition is the author's; a supporter of wokeism will object that property 3 is a caricature and that rights without support are empty, an opponent that property 4 is understated. The poll figures are from memory. The chapter examines neither the content of the demands nor their justice: only how they are built as a mechanism and what a price does to them. 🟡
