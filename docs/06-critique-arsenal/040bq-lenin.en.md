# 40bq. Vladimir Lenin: Ten Theses — "The Lower Classes Do Not Want" as a Measurable Quantity, the Cook Gets a Choice, Consciousness without a Party, and the Lesson of the Dispersed Assembly

**Chapter:** 06 — The Structural Hypocrisy of Criticism and the Arsenal of the Elites
**File:** 06_040bq · v1 · 30 October 2026
**Source:** the architect's question of 30 October 2026 ("whom have we not examined yet — perhaps Lenin?"). Vladimir Lenin: *What Is to Be Done?* (1902), *Two Tactics of Social-Democracy* (1905), *Imperialism, the Highest Stage of Capitalism* (1916), *The Collapse of the Second International* (1915; "the upper classes cannot, the lower classes do not want"), *Can the Bolsheviks Retain State Power?* (1917; "every cook"), *The State and Revolution* (1917), *On Compromises* (1917), the decree dissolving the Constituent Assembly (January 1918). Before this chapter Lenin appeared in the repository only in Bannon's quotation "I am a Leninist" ([040ak](040ak-bannon.md)); communism as a subject — [040ah](040ah-communism.md), [040ai](040ai-how-communism-arises.md).

*How to read. Lenin is the one in the series who did not theorise about elections but took power and, first thing, dispersed the assembly in which he had lost. He therefore has two voices, the theorist's and the practitioner's, and they strike the protocol from different sides. The chapter checks ten of his theses by one yardstick and examines three blows: "the same labour aristocracy, only for everyone", "I will disperse it like the Assembly", "you paid the poorest for silence".*

## 1. Ten theses

| No. | Lenin's thesis | What is true today | What the protocol does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A revolutionary situation: "the upper classes cannot, the lower classes do not want" (1915) | The condition is not measured; it is seen after the fact, when bodies are already in the street | The share of takers by district every cycle ([048m art. 9](../08-implementation/048m-charter.md)) measures "the lower classes do not want" in real time; the threshold is visible before it is crossed ([057h §2.3](../09-other-countries/057h-serbia-2026.md): things need not get very bad) |
| 2 | Consciousness does not arise spontaneously; a party of professionals brings it from outside (1902) | Every project of change requires an organisation ([40bg](040bg-q-change-needs-organization.md)) | The protocol needs no organisation: there is nothing to bring, the price sorts by itself; everyone learns his own stake without an agitator and without a party |
| 3 | "Every cook must learn to govern the state" (1917) | The cook is brought to vote, not invited to govern | The cook gets a choice for the first time: money or governing; the one who stays weighs double and counts the departments first ([019c §6k](../04-electoral-dynamics/019c-reform-by-subtraction.md)) |
| 4 | Parliament is a "talking shop"; go to the Duma only to expose it (1906–1912) | A parliament without a stake is indeed a talking shop: it does not decide | A hall with a stake needs no exposing — it asks for the number itself (019c §6k); the talking ends where the listener holds a receipt |
| 5 | The Constituent Assembly dispersed after a lost election (January 1918) | The main practical lesson: whoever controls the count and the hall is not bound by elections | The protocol makes the count self-controlling ([048i](../08-implementation/048i-three-channels-parallel-count.md), art. 10: records = votes + payments + neither); an assembly can be dispersed, money paid to millions of cards cannot be recalled ([057d §3b](../09-other-countries/057d-venezuela.md)) |
| 6 | "The worse, the better": change requires a crisis | Serbia confirmed it: change came only when things got very bad (057h §2.3) | The protocol changes at a small deterioration: the share of takers shifts before bodies fall; crisis stops being the condition |
| 7 | The "labour aristocracy" is bought off and votes for the status quo (1916) | The bought vote exists and is hidden in benefits and indexations ([057h §2.6](../09-other-countries/057h-serbia-2026.md)) | The protocol puts a ceiling and a receipt on the purchase ([1g](../01-introduction/001g-objections-index.md); [040bm §2](040bm-buchanan.md)); the buying becomes open, equal and voluntary |
| 8 | The state is a machine for the suppression of one class by another (1917) | The apparatus serves whoever feeds it | The one budget line the elected do not touch goes not to the apparatus but to the citizen (art. 14; [040bm §5](040bm-buchanan.md)); the machine acquires an owner with a receipt |
| 9 | Compromises are admissible as tactics; principles are not traded (1917) | Politics lives by packages and deals | One formula and fourteen articles are not traded; only p is, and only by referendum (art. 3) |
| 10 | Dual power: two centres of legitimacy in one country (1917) | Turnout and real support diverge, and nobody knows by how much | The three states of art. 10 show the divergence as a number; dual power becomes visible before it becomes street-level ([013g](../03-theory/013g-decay-of-democracy.md)) |

## 2. The first blow, the theorist's: "you are buying the worker — it is the same labour aristocracy, only for everyone"

Lenin's argument of 1916: the bourgeoisie shares its super-profits with the top layer of workers, and they vote for order. The protocol, he will say, extends the bribe to everyone. The answer is a fork ([proof by contradiction](040bj-machiavelli.md)). Either buying the voter is an evil, and then the present buying — hidden in benefits, targeted and without a ceiling — is worse than an open, equal one with a receipt; the protocol is honest accounting of what is already done ([1g](../01-introduction/001g-objections-index.md): "A payment from taxes"). Or buying is not an evil, and the objection removes itself. The argument has no third option. And the subject differs: the labour aristocracy was chosen by the payer; under the protocol each chooses himself (art. 5) — not a bribe from above but a share the citizen may take or leave ([057d §3b](../09-other-countries/057d-venezuela.md): for the first time nothing need be given up for the share).

## 3. The second blow, the practitioner's: "whoever controls the count is not bound by your protocol — I will disperse it like the Assembly"

This is the strongest blow, because it is not theoretical: it was done in January 1918. The answer: the Constituent Assembly could be dispersed because its result was paper and people in one building. The protocol's result is money on millions of cards and a record held by every citizen of what he chose, reconciled with the payment (art. 10; [048i](../08-implementation/048i-three-channels-parallel-count.md): three channels of counting). The payment cannot be recalled ([057d §3b](../09-other-countries/057d-venezuela.md): the share that cannot be revoked); the rule can be repealed only by a referendum in which half the country votes against its own money ([048g](../08-implementation/048g-rollback.md)); and lowering p is a kamikaze ([059l §8b](../10-usa/059l-five-thousand-rehearsal.md)). Lenin dispersed an assembly that had not a single rouble in the voter's pocket. The honest residue: force can cancel anything, and the protocol is no defence against a coup ([040i](040i-linz-shulman.md): repression is not an objection to the rule); it is a defence against what Lenin did legally — the cancellation of a result that cost nobody money.

## 4. The third blow: "your hall is petty-bourgeois — those with something to lose; you paid the poorest for silence"

Lenin will say the protocol leaves the propertied in the hall and takes the proletarian out with money. The answer is [040bp §2](040bp-gumilev.md): below the median, where D is weighty by definition (art. 3.3), the one who stays has a drive exceeding gain by the whole of D. That is his "conscious worker", only found by a price, not by a party; and his consciousness is revealed by behaviour, not attributed by an agitator. And the one who took did not fall silent — he made a choice that is recorded and counted (art. 10), and he returns when the stake exceeds the money ([1h](../01-introduction/001h-glossary.md): states, not types). Silence is today's turnout of 40–50 %, of which nobody knows what it means; the protocol gives that silence a number and a price.

## 5. What Lenin gives the protocol

The formula for the condition of change — "the lower classes do not want" — which the protocol makes a measurable quantity for the first time, rather than a diagnosis after the fact. The cook as the name for a citizen who is for the first time offered to govern, not only to vote. The practitioner's honest admission that elections are decided by whoever controls the count — which is why in the protocol the count is given not to a commission but to the choice and the payment themselves. And the lesson of January 1918: a result that cost nobody money is cancelled by decree; a result paid to a card is not.

## 6. Summary

| Thesis | What is true | Protocol | What remains |
|---|---|---|---|
| the upper cannot, the lower do not want | yes | measured every cycle | — |
| consciousness from outside | no: the price sorts without a party | — | — |
| the cook | yes | gets a choice, not only a vote | — |
| the talking shop | yes, without a stake | a hall with a stake asks for the number | — |
| the dispersed assembly | the lesson holds | a result in money is not recalled | force cancels everything; no defence against a coup |
| the worse, the better | confirmed by Serbia | change at a small deterioration | — |
| the labour aristocracy | yes, buying exists | a ceiling and a receipt; one chooses oneself | the cost is explicit, not zero (040bm) |
| the state as a machine | yes | a line to the citizen, not the apparatus | — |
| compromises | yes | the formula is not traded, p by referendum | — |
| dual power | yes | visible as a number before the street | — |

## Weak point

Lenin wrote about seizing power, not measuring it; the chapter is easily read as "the protocol against revolution", whereas it only makes the condition of revolution visible in advance — and thereby removes the need for it. Quotations are paraphrased; "every cook" in the original "must learn to govern", not "can govern", and the chapter takes Lenin's sense, not the popular one. The context of 1917 — war, collapse, open voting in the factories — does not transfer one to one to elections by secret ballot. The answer to the second blow holds for cancelling a result by legal means and does not hold for force: that is the boundary the repository keeps everywhere ([040i](040i-linz-shulman.md)).
