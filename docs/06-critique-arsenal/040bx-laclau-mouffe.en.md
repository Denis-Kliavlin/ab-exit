# 40bx. Ernesto Laclau and Chantal Mouffe: Five Theses — the People Are Constructed, the Empty Signifier, Antagonism as the Essence of the Political; and Why an Assembly of "Againsts" Does Not Hold Where the Vote Has a Price

**Chapter:** 06 — The Structural Hypocrisy of Criticism and the Arsenal of the Elites
**File:** 06_040bx · v1 · 8 November 2026
**Source:** the architect's question of 7 November 2026 ("the professor of militant populism — is he that smart, or what?"). Ernesto Laclau: *On Populist Reason* (2005); Laclau and Mouffe: *Hegemony and Socialist Strategy* (1985); Chantal Mouffe: *On the Political* (2005), *For a Left Populism* (2018). The only ones in the series who defend populism as a method rather than diagnose it; quotations are paraphrased.

---

*How to read. Laclau and Mouffe described what a populist majority is made of more honestly than any critic of populism: heterogeneous grievances, an empty symbol, an enemy. The chapter tests five theses and three blows: "the people must be constructed", "antagonism is ineradicable", "without a leader-signifier there is no politics". Their recipe is written for a hall with a free vote; the chapter shows that it loses its material where the vote has a price.*

## 1. Five theses

| No. | Laclau and Mouffe's thesis | What is true today | What the protocol does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | There is no "people"; it is constructed: a chain of equivalences — heterogeneous demands linked against a common enemy | True as a description of the assembly; not of its service life: a coalition assembled on "against" has no contract, and its first decision must betray someone ([059p §3b](../10-usa/059p-third-force-demand.md)) | Whoever came only "against" takes the payment or votes for a third candidate; the stayer demands a "for"; the chain snaps on day one, because ambiguity stops paying ([1i](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)) |
| 2 | The empty signifier: a leader or slogan into which everyone reads his own meaning | "They voted for very different Trumps" — strategic ambiguity that pays while the voter does not check ([059p](../10-usa/059p-third-force-demand.md)) | A hall with a price checks; the rule too has a slogan with four readings ("the vote is worth nothing — today"), but behind it is a number, one for all, and a number cannot be read into |
| 3 | Antagonism is the essence of the political; "us against them" is ineradicable; Mouffe: turn enemies into adversaries (agonism) through a culture of debate | Yes, "against" is always there; half a century of debate culture has not turned it into anything | "Against" gets an addressee with a name and a price (1i); the enemy becomes a taker — he left by himself; one does not fight the one who took: he removed himself from the board by his own choice. Agonism is achieved by design, not by upbringing |
| 4 | Institutions cannot absorb demands; the unabsorbed accumulate and explode | Their strongest point: these are the burned, and "the lower classes do not want" as a quantity ([040bq thesis 1](040bq-lenin.md); [040bw](040bw-pape.md)) | In Laclau the accumulation is visible only by the explosion; under the rule the share who took shows it as a number every cycle, before the street |
| 5 | Populism is not a content but a logic; hence it can be left or right | Agrees with Mudde ("thin ideology", [040ab](040ab-mudde.md)) | The rule is neither left nor right: one lever cuts the electorate across, not along, and it cannot be captured (1i) |

## 2. The first blow: "the people must be constructed; without a chain of equivalences and a signifier there is no majority"

The answer is a fork. Accept it whole: a majority exists only as a linkage of heterogeneous "againsts" around an empty symbol. Then it exists exactly until the first decision — and 2026 showed it: a coalition assembled that way collapsed the next cycle, and the next one was assembled the same way ([059o](../10-usa/059o-ship-turning-every-700-days.md), [059p](../10-usa/059p-third-force-demand.md)). Either the argument is false and a majority can be assembled otherwise; or it is true — and then it describes not politics but the free vote: linking free "againsts" costs nothing, which is why it holds until the first price. Under the rule the people are not constructed — they assemble themselves by selection at a price, with no leader, no signifier and no enemy: each compared two numbers and stayed ([040bo §3](040bo-montaigne.md)). That is what Laclau wanted of "the people" — a subject, not an object — only found by price, not by discourse.

## 3. The second blow: "antagonism is ineradicable; your hall without an enemy is not politics but bookkeeping"

True: a hall without an enemy is bookkeeping, and the repository calls it that ([the accounting standard](040br-ten-propaganda-devices.md)). The difference is that bookkeeping does not abolish antagonism but gives it an addressee and a price: "against" in the rule is choice B, the line that cannot be struck off ([1g](../01-introduction/001g-objections-index.md)), and the third candidate of the centre (1i). Mouffe wanted "agonism" — adversaries instead of enemies — and proposed to cultivate it; the rule makes it a design: the one who took is not an enemy, he left; the one who stayed argues with one who also stayed and also counted. Antagonism remains — it acquires an account.

## 4. The third blow: "without a leader-signifier there is no assembly; nobody will carry your rule"

The answer is in [048n](../08-implementation/048n-underdog-questions.md): the carrier is a political technologist with a product, not a party with a conviction; and "service" — the carrier carries the fire, not himself. Laclau is right that the first needs a person with a name; the repository answers that the name is needed once — a referendum adopts the rule, not the carrier ([048m art. 14](../08-implementation/048m-charter.md)), and after the first launch the number becomes public and the rest come by themselves ([1i: the underdog wins whatever the referendum's outcome](../01-introduction/001i-consequences.md)). The rule's signifier is not empty: it is a sum with a date.

## 5. What Laclau and Mouffe give the protocol

An honest anatomy of the populist majority — what it is assembled from — which the critics of populism describe worse. An explanation of why the coalitions of 2024 and 2026 do not hold: not a vice of the people but a property of the free vote. And the term "agonism", for which the rule supplies a design instead of an upbringing.

## 6. Summary

| Thesis | What is true | Protocol | What remains |
|---|---|---|---|
| the people are constructed | the assembly — yes | the people assemble themselves by price | real disagreements remain |
| the empty signifier | yes, while nobody checks | a number cannot be read into | — |
| antagonism | yes | gets an addressee and a price | — |
| unabsorbed demands | yes | visible as a number before the explosion | — |
| logic, not content | yes | the rule is neither left nor right | — |

## Weak point

Laclau and Mouffe work in terms of discourse, not elections; the translation of their theses into the language of turnout and price is the author's, and they could reply that a price is also a signifier. "Smart, or what" in the architect's question — the answer: smart in the anatomy of the assembly, blind to the fact that the assembly is free and therefore short-lived. The chapter does not examine their theory of hegemony as a whole — only what concerns the hall.
