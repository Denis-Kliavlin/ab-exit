## 40.2. Grok's verdict (verbatim, final wording)

> "1. Within its own logic the protocol contains no structural contradiction that brings it down. The previous hundreds of rounds did not find one either. 2. The mechanism is coherent and better protected against a number of classic objections than most reforms that get proposed. 3. It is worth piloting in one region/state with open data — the theoretical case is strong enough, and the cost of a failed pilot is bounded. This is not equal to the claim 'it will be better than now'. 'It will be better' is an empirical question. [...] The logic is not broken. The pilot is justified. One cannot claim 'it will definitely be better' — that goes beyond what the stress test showed."

## 40.4. The refusal as a signature under the honesty of the test

Essential: Grok in the dialogue **refused** to extend the acknowledgement to "it will be better than now" ("No. I did not acknowledge that") — and drew the categorial boundary correctly: a stress test of logic in principle cannot establish an empirical outcome. An attacker pressed to "it will be better" would have devalued the round (persuaded, rather than "could not break it"); one who dug in puts an expert's signature under the test protocol. The boundary "internal consistency ≠ proven beneficence", drawn in the project by the author and co-author earlier, has now been drawn by an adversary — and has become common ground.

## 40.5. Grok's six unknowns = the official KPIs of the pilot

The list of empirical uncertainties named by Grok is accepted by the project as **the technical specification for the pilot's measurements** (the metrics were formulated by the adversary — a defence against the accusation of convenient self-metrics):

1. Real exit rates (a check of the computed 55–65 % structure)
2. The legitimacy reaction (perception of a government elected at the new turnout)
3. The strength of stigma (the social meanings of choice B — the Gaaze residue, §038)
4. Soft pressure by the state and third parties (Anti-Coercion in practice)
5. Fiscal sustainability (the load at actual exit)
6. The behaviour of the remaining 35–45 % of voters (signal quality, the §034 balance)

Note: Grok's wording "the remaining 35–45 %" uses the computed ranges of thesis 1 as working ones — the attacker accepted the exit structure at least as the base model.
