What is arithmetic, and what is a hypothesis?

Arithmetic: every group sums to 10,000; AB-EXIT pays only expected B participants; the A-vote weight is the total electorate divided by remaining candidate voters.

Hypothesis: turnout, support, A/B choice, and producing-core responses come from a versioned synthetic profile and require field calibration. Acceptance is not hard-coded: within the controls above, the model can return PASSED, REJECTED, or NO QUORUM.
