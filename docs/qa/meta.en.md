### Q-META-001 · What does "449+ rounds, 0 contradictions" mean? Is that proof?

**Status:** 🔁 contested
**Who asks:** academics, journalists, sceptics — the first thing they ask
**Source in the book:** [README](https://github.com/Denis-Kliavlin/ab-exit#ai-stress-test-results), [§43 "Gemini's capitulation"](../08-implementation/043-gemini-capitulation.md), [index of transcripts](https://github.com/Denis-Kliavlin/ab-exit/blob/main/en/transcripts/INDEX.md)
**Related:** Q-META-002, Q-META-003

**Answer.** It is not proof of truth, and the book does not claim that. ChatGPT's formulation
after the formal stress test is exact: *within the model's stated assumptions no internal
logical contradiction was found that would collapse the mechanism on its own foundations*.
That is, what was tested was **internal consistency**, not correspondence to reality. Six
architectures (Claude, Gemini, Grok, Qwen, DeepSeek, ChatGPT), 10 sessions, transcripts
published in full in a court-record format.

**Weak point of the answer.** LLMs are poorly suited to the role of adversary: they tend to agree
with an insistent interlocutor, and §45 item 68.10 itself records the limit of LLM adversarial
assessment — on legal questions the third round exposed risks absent from the first two.
"No model broke it" is an argument about the quality of the question no more than about the
quality of the opponent. Live academic peer review is not replaced by those 449 rounds.

### Q-META-002 · What exactly is the $10,000 question and why can it not be answered?

**Status:** ✅ answered
**Who asks:** everyone
**Source in the book:** [ABEXIT_Question_v4.3](https://github.com/Denis-Kliavlin/ab-exit/blob/main/en/docs/ABEXIT_Question_v4.3.md)
**Related:** Q-META-001, Q-LEG-001

**Answer.** The formulation: *if 48 % already do not vote — for free — and the state already
pays parties for the votes they receive, why is it criminal to pay a citizen for an honest
"I do not want to participate", when all his other rights remain?*

Ten logical filters are attached to the question: an answer counts only if it passes all of them.
The $10,000 prize is announced publicly and has not been paid.

**Weak point of the answer.** A prize with filters that the author himself formulates and judges is
a rhetorical construction, not open verification. A strong answer can be rejected as "failing
filter N". For the question to work as a scientific wager, the judging must be external.

### Q-META-003 · What is the document's status: academic work, manifesto or draft law?

**Status:** ✅ answered
**Who asks:** academics, editors, legislators
**Source in the book:** [index](../index.md), [§49 "The legal charter"](../10-usa/049-legal-statute.md), [LICENSE](https://github.com/Denis-Kliavlin/ab-exit/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
**Related:** Q-META-001

**Answer.** All three layers are present and separated by part. Parts I–III are the analytical
and philosophical layer. Part IV is applied: §49 carries the construction through to the text
of a charter. Parts I–V together are published as a book (v6.57) at docs.ab-exit.com. The licence
is dual, on the Torvalds model: free for citizens, activists and NGOs, paid for commercial use.

**Weak point of the answer.** The mixing of genres hurts reception: an academic sees a manifesto and
does not review it; a legislator sees philosophy and does not take it up. A separate academic
digest for peer review has not been extracted.

### Q-META-004 · Why this Q&A base, if there is a book?

**Status:** ✅ answered
**Who asks:** contributors
**Source in the book:** [introduction to the base](index.md)
**Related:** —

**Answer.** The book answers at length and linearly, while objections arrive pointwise and
repeat. The base is an entry index: "this question has come up, here is the short answer,
here is where to read in detail". Its second purpose is **an honest account of open places**:
the field "weak point of the answer" is mandatory in every entry, and status 🟡 means an
acknowledged question without an answer. An open question recorded in the base is more useful
than an absent one.

**Weak point of the answer.** The base lives only if it is replenished. If new objections are worked
through in correspondence and do not land here, it will go stale and become harmful —
creating the impression that everything has been accounted for.
