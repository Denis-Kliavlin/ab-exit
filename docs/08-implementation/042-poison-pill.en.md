### 73.1. Purpose of the section

When the system cannot defeat AB-EXIT in open battle, it will try to lead it and distort it. This is the most dangerous attack — public capitulation with quiet sabotage through a change of one variable in the formula. The section records four known ways of such substitution so that they are recognised in advance and blocked at the stage of drafting the statute. Each way sounds like "a small technical correction" but in fact destroys the protocol.

### 73.2. Way 1: substituting the source with budget revenue (General Fund Revenue)

**The "improvement" proposal:** "The city is a corporation; the dividend should depend on its revenue. Let's count from General Fund tax revenue, not from some W-2 wages."

**The hidden poison:** the mayor himself controls tax rates. Raises taxes by 20 % → budget revenue grows → the dividend grows → the mayor looks like a hero. In reality business flees the city, people get poorer. The protocol rewards the mayor for strangling the economy.

**Protection:** fix the source in the statute immovably as the W-2 median wage (external SSA data), which the mayor does not control. No references to municipal finances as the base.

### 73.3. Way 2: substituting the source with the Census

**The "improvement" proposal:** "The Census Bureau counts households better; let's use Census data."

**The hidden poison:** the fine for lying in Census surveys is zero dollars. Nobody has ever gone to prison for it. If the dividend is tied to a survey, people will start massively inflating income in questionnaires for a bigger payment, and the data source breaks. An autoimmune disease of the protocol: the system destroys its own base.

**Comparison with the W-2:** forging a W-2 — up to 5 years in prison, a 97 % probability of conviction. It is a criminally protected source, which makes mass falsification economically unprofitable.

**Protection:** only the W-2, no surveys, no self-declared data.

### 73.4. Way 3: dilution through a composite index

**The "improvement" proposal:** "Orienting on wages alone is narrow. Let's make a comprehensive index: 40 % wages, 20 % safety, 25 % ecology, 15 % education."

**The hidden poison:** as soon as the formula splits into weights, the mayor starts manipulating. He lobbies for a bigger weight for the metric where he happens to have good indicators, and the dividend stops reflecting the city's real wellbeing. Cumbersome "fairness" kills simple feedback.

**Protection:** one metric — the W-2 median. Simplicity is not a bug but a feature: it cannot be manipulated through lobbying the weights.

### 73.5. Way 4: killing the automaticity (a barrier at exit)

**The "improvement" proposal:** "To protect against fraudsters, pay the $780 only in cash at the polling station" / "through Equity Board approval" / "with a union veto" / "after an interview with a commission".

**The hidden poison:** the most inconspicuous and most deadly distortion. The critical design element — the dividend is credited AUTOMATICALLY, by pressing a button, without visits and queues. The barrier at exit must equal zero. If the apathetic is made to travel to the polling station, stand in a queue or pass a commission for the money, he will wave his hand and out of habit poke at random on the ballot. The filter breaks, the apathetic stay in the system, the protocol does not work.

**Protection:** automatic crediting to a bank account, tied to voter-registration status. No commissions, no in-person procedures.

### 73.6. The general principle of protection

In the text of the citizen-initiated statute five parameters must be fixed immovably, whose change requires a new referendum, not a decision of the legislature:

1. Data source: the W-2 median from the SSA. Only.
2. Formula: D = M × 1.5 × 1 %. Without additional coefficients and indices.
3. Method of payment: automatic crediting to a bank account.
4. Reversibility cycle: every 4 years the choice is made anew.
5. Absence of preconditions: no commissions, no approvals, no vetoes.

Any attempt to change even one of these five parameters through the ordinary legislative process is a "poison pill" attempt. This protection must be built into the text of the initiative itself as a self-protecting clause: changing these parameters is possible only through a new citizen initiative with the same signature threshold.
