### 94.1. Purpose of the section

Modern elections are subjected to ever more sophisticated methods of interference — from foreign states, corporate interests, political parties. Traditional methods of defence (legal regulation of AI, fact-checking, media literacy) systematically lag behind the technologies and are ineffective in modern conditions. This section shows that AB-EXIT is the first structural answer to the problem of interference that works independently of technology, through a change in the composition of the electorate.

This is a structural advantage that is not immediately obvious from the basic concept of AB-EXIT, but which substantially strengthens its value in the era of AI interference in elections.

### 94.2. Six methods of election interference in 2024–2026

An empirical systematisation of the methods based on data from the last two electoral cycles:

**First — AI-generated deepfakes.** In January 2024 in New Hampshire a robocall with an AI voice of Biden went out, urging people not to take part in the primary. In Slovakia audio deepfakes about alleged electoral fraud spread. In the USA before the 2024 election a deepfake audio of Harris circulated on TikTok, fake audio of Trump mocking Republicans. More than 130 deepfakes were identified in elections worldwide since September 2023. According to the World Economic Forum Global Risks 2024–2025, disinformation is considered a significant threat to humanity.

**Second — foreign interference by adversary states.** Russia, China, Iran actively sought to influence the outcome of the 2024 US presidential election. The Treasury Department tracked money flows to the Nashville-based company Tenet Media, which spread Russian narratives through bot farms and pro-Russian influencers. Three cyber-operatives of the Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) were indicted. In Australia before the May 2025 election a Russian-linked influence network published fake articles.

**Third — data-poisoning attacks on AI chatbots (a new method of 2025).** Before the UK 2024 election roughly 1 in 8 voters turned to AI chatbots for political answers. Before the Australian election of May 2025 a Russian-linked network published thousands of fake articles not for people directly but to train AI search crawlers — distorting the data on which these tools build their answers, in favour of Russian interests. An attack on the tools voters use.

**Fourth — social networks as vectors of manipulation.** By 2024 X (formerly Twitter) and TikTok had become the main vectors for disinformation, especially for spreading AI-generated deepfakes. Bot farms, microtargeting through AI, coordinated campaigns to amplify particular narratives.

**Fifth — smear campaigns as the dominant type.** Smear campaigns — deepfake content accusing candidates of controversial but fabricated activities — were the most common category of AI disinformation in 2024 in the USA. Deepfakes showed candidates swearing, using firearms, consuming drugs or suffering from critical conditions.

**Sixth — accusations as a weapon (the meta-level).** Even the accusation of using AI (true or false) becomes a political tool. In New York accusations were made both against Zohran Mamdani in the mayoral primary and against the Democratic candidate Blake Gendebien. The very existence of deepfake technology becomes a weapon — accusations discredit any material, real or not.

### 94.3. The goals of all these methods

— **Suppressing the turnout** of particular demographic groups (the fake Biden robocalls urge people not to vote). — **Intensifying political polarisation** through targeted emotional content. — **Smearing candidates** through fabricated controversies. — **Amplifying conspiracy theories** after real events (for example, after the attempt on Trump in July 2024). — **Annulling election results** — the most extreme case, empirically recorded in Romania 2024.

### 94.4. The key structural observation

All six methods have **one common structural feature**. They work through **the apathetic, emotionally manipulable mass of voters**.

This is not an interpretation; it is an empirically recorded fact. Disinformation disproportionately affects groups usually subjected to manipulative content through racist, misogynistic, xenophobic and other emotional triggers. AI allows high proportions of tailored content for different demographic groups.

Pragmatists and Ideologues (section 71 of our document) are far less susceptible to these methods for structural reasons:

— **Pragmatists** check figures and facts through several sources. Their decisions rest on calculations, not emotions. A deepfake without verification does not affect their choice.

— **Ideologues** have stable value systems. Their choice is determined by principles, not by reaction to specific content. Emotional mobilisation against their values does not work.

— **The apathetic mass** is the main target of disinformation campaigns. It does not check, is easily mobilised by emotions, has no stable values for filtering manipulation.

That is, **the methods of election interference structurally work through the same vulnerability that AB-EXIT removes**. This is not a random coincidence — it is a structural link between the nature of the problem and the nature of the solution.

### 94.5. How AB-EXIT disarms each of the six methods

**Deepfakes.** Created for the emotional mobilisation of emotionally manipulable voters. After AB-EXIT these voters took the dividend and left the electoral field. At the polls remain Pragmatists (who will check the deepfake through fact-checkers) and Ideologues (who will reject it on the basis of values). The effectiveness of deepfakes falls from mass impact to near zero, because there is no audience that resonates with them.

**Turnout suppression through robocalls.** The method works by persuading those on the edge between "vote or not" not to vote. After AB-EXIT these people have already taken the dividend — they lawfully do not vote by their own choice. A robocall cannot persuade someone not to vote who does not vote anyway. And Pragmatists and Ideologues do not listen to robocalls. The method loses its whole function.

**Foreign interference.** The main weapon of Russia, China, Iran is mobilising the apathetic mass through social networks for particular narratives. Without the apathetic mass as an audience there is no attack vector. Bot farms may continue to publish, but their content finds no resonant audience. Foreign interference becomes structurally powerless.

**Data poisoning of AI chatbots.** The attack counts on voters using AI for decisions (1 in 8 in the UK 2024). This behaviour is characteristic precisely of those who do not do their own analysis — i.e. the category that after AB-EXIT will take the dividend. Pragmatists do not trust AI in political questions; they trust their own analysis. Ideologues rely on values. The attack has no target.

**Smear campaigns.** Work through the emotional arousal of the mass — "look at the candidate's terrible act". Pragmatists immediately check the source. Ideologues demand proof. The emotionally manipulable mass that would previously have resonated is now at home with the dividend. Smear campaigns are confined to a politically active audience capable of verification.

**Microtargeting and social networks.** The whole microtargeting infrastructure is designed for mobilising the apathetic mobilisable mass. Pragmatists and Ideologues are structurally not susceptible to microtargeting — they have their own sources of analysis and stable values. The microtargeting industry loses its market — it structurally becomes unprofitable for political campaigns.

**Accusations as a weapon.** This method requires voters to react emotionally to any accusation without going into the details. Pragmatists check the details. Ideologues demand context. The method loses its force through the same structural cause.

### 94.6. Romania 2024 as proof of the helplessness of the current system

The annulment of the results of Romania's 2024 presidential election because of AI interference is **an admission of defeat by the current system of defending democracy**. The democratic system could not defend itself from AI manipulation. The results had to be cancelled — the most dramatic step a democratic system can take.

This is empirical confirmation that **current methods of defence do not work**:

— Legal regulation of AI lags behind the technologies. — Fact-checking does not catch up with the speed of spread. — Voter education does not solve the problem within one electoral cycle. — Platform moderation is circumvented through bot farms. — State investigations act post factum, when it is already too late.

And Romania is not an isolated case. It is a symptom of a structural problem. AI technologies develop faster than democratic systems can adapt to them.

### 94.7. Comparison with traditional methods of defence against interference

An analysis of existing approaches to election defence:

**Legal regulation of AI (the EU AI Act, US executive orders).** Advantage — a formal framework. Disadvantage — lags behind the technologies by years. By the time regulation is adopted the technology has already evolved. The EU AI Act was adopted in 2024; the technologies of 2026 are already different.

**Fact-checking.** Advantage — precise correction of specific deepfakes. Disadvantage — catastrophically behind in speed. A deepfake spreads to millions in hours; a fact-check takes days, and by the time of publication many will never return to the article for the correction.

**Media literacy.** Advantage — a long-term solution. Disadvantage — requires generations for a substantial effect. Does not help the current election.

**Platform moderation.** Advantage — scalability. Disadvantage — circumvented through bot farms, fake accounts, foreign infrastructure. Every tightening of moderation gives a temporary effect.

**State investigations.** Advantage — punishment of the guilty. Disadvantage — post factum, the election is already over. The Tenet Media indictment came after the content had already influenced perception of the election.

**AB-EXIT as a structural defence.** Advantage — works **independently of technology**, because it changes the audience rather than blocking content. Deepfake technology can develop as much as it likes — without an audience that resonates with it, it is structurally powerless. This is **the only method of defence that does not require catching up with the technologies**.

This means that AB-EXIT is a **long-term structural solution**, unlike tactical solutions (regulation, fact-checking, education). Tactical solutions require constant adaptation. A structural solution works once and keeps acting.
