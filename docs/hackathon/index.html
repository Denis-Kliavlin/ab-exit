AB-EXIT · OpenAI Build Week

A patch for democracy.

AB-EXIT is designed to improve the quality of executive government by changing electoral incentives—not by replacing democracy. It adds one voluntary choice to existing elections, so politicians must compete for a smaller, more engaged electorate while every citizen receives a meaningful option.

A · Keep your voteVote for candidates as usual. When others choose B, fewer ballots decide the result—so each remaining vote carries more relative weight.
B · Take the paymentReceive a transparent public payment and give up voting only in this election cycle. Your voting rights return at the next election.

The goal: better candidates, longer-term decisions, and a more accountable executive branch. The mechanism: citizens choose immediate personal value or greater political influence. These four simulations test the mechanism; they do not treat the promised improvement as already proven.
